# PAPI MARKETING & GROWTH 1.37

## Scope
Read-only analysis of explicitly supplied campaign snapshots: impressions, clicks, leads, orders, declared spend and revenue-evidence label. Calculates funnel ratios, cost per lead and conditional ROAS. Groups results by channel and proposes zero-budget experiments.

## Privacy
Only campaign-level aggregate metrics are accepted. Unknown fields are rejected; do not submit names, phone numbers, email addresses, audience exports, private messages or customer-level records.

## Financial interpretation
Amounts are XOF. Declared revenue is not treated as verified. ROAS is displayed only when revenue_evidence_status is marked provider_confirmed, but that label itself is caller-supplied and is not independently authenticated by this module.

## Safety
The module does not connect to social networks, publish content, send messages, launch ads, contact prospects, create accounts, spend funds or change prices. Suggested experiments are proposals only; monthly_budget_xof is a planning input and does not authorize spending. The initial and default budget is 0 FCFA.
