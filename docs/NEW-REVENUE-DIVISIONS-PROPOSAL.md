# New revenue divisions and software apprenticeship proposal

Status: proposal for review. This document does not activate agents, connect services, spend money, submit applications, enter contests, or authorize resale of third-party products.

## Objectives

Add three opportunities to the PAPI COMMERCE AI business plan:

1. A lawful digital-voucher and promotional-offer research activity.
2. A software-building apprentice reporting to Ibrahima, able to deliver software, SaaS products, websites and applications with minimal owner intervention.
3. A dedicated competitions, prizes and rewards research department.

All agents remain subject to PAPI identity, hierarchy, least-privilege permissions, audit logging, policy checks and financial approval rules.

## A. Digital promotions, vouchers and coupon intelligence

### Proposed team
- **Promotion Research Agent**: searches official brand, publisher, telecom, gaming and subscription sources for free offers, coupon codes, trial offers, gift cards, bundles and limited-time promotions.
- **Offer Verification Agent**: checks source authenticity, expiry, territory, eligibility, redemption limits, transferability, account binding and terms of use.
- **Voucher Inventory Agent**: records each offer, source URL, discovery date, expiry, region, restrictions, proof of terms, verification status and stock/quantity where applicable.
- **Commercial Partnerships Agent**: seeks authorized affiliate, reseller, referral or distribution arrangements with issuers and merchants.
- **Offer Support Agent**: explains redemption conditions and handles customer questions without collecting passwords or one-time authentication codes.

### Allowed business models
- Affiliate commissions and official referral rewards.
- Authorized resale or distribution of vouchers explicitly marked transferable/resellable by the issuer.
- Paid discovery/curation service for legitimate discounts and deals.
- Merchant-funded promotional campaigns and lead generation, with disclosure.

### Prohibited activity
- Reselling codes, subscriptions, trials, accounts or benefits when the issuer's terms prohibit resale or transfer.
- Selling account-bound, personalized, single-use, region-locked or non-transferable codes as if they were transferable.
- Account theft, credential sharing, payment fraud, exploiting bugs, bypassing eligibility, generating fake identities, or abusing referral programs.
- Advertising a code as valid without verification or hiding restrictions.
- Buying inventory, paying for subscriptions, or making any financial commitment without the required owner authorization.

### Verification workflow
1. Discover offer from an official issuer or reputable authorized partner.
2. Preserve the exact source and terms.
3. Classify: free public code / personal code / transferable voucher / affiliate offer / trial / uncertain.
4. Verify geography, expiry, eligible users, redemption limits and transfer rights.
5. Reject or quarantine uncertain, suspicious or non-transferable offers.
6. Calculate net margin only for lawful authorized resale or commission models.
7. Publish only with accurate terms, timestamp and status; recheck expiry before sale.

### Key metrics
Verified offers found; percentage still valid at redemption; authorized transferable inventory; conversion rate; gross and net margin; refunds/complaints; fraud rate; time-to-expiry losses.

## B. Software engineering apprentice reporting to Ibrahima

### Proposed role
**Software Engineering Apprentice / Product Builder**, reporting directly to **Ibrahima – Architecture and Agent Development Lead**.

The apprentice should be capable of independently:
- Researching software requirements and validating a product opportunity.
- Designing and implementing websites, web applications, mobile applications, APIs, internal tools and SaaS products.
- Creating UI/UX, database schemas, authentication, role-based access, billing integrations (in test mode until authorized), documentation and deployment plans.
- Writing automated tests, fixing defects, reviewing security, monitoring performance and maintaining products.
- Preparing demos, technical proposals, estimates and portfolio evidence.
- Finding suitable paid freelance/software missions, evaluating scope and eligibility, and preparing applications and proposals.

### Autonomy model
- May independently research, plan, code, test, document and create draft deliverables in approved repositories and sandboxes.
- May autonomously identify suitable freelance opportunities and prepare tailored applications using only truthful, owner-approved profile facts and portfolio evidence.
- May submit applications only where the owner has explicitly pre-authorized the platform, profile, permitted service categories, rate/price floor, workload limits and standard terms. Otherwise prepare the complete application for approval.
- Must never invent qualifications, clients, employment history, references, portfolio results or delivery capacity.
- Must not accept binding contracts, incur expenses, purchase tools, subscribe to services, disclose secrets, or access client production systems without explicit authorization.
- Must request approval before committing to deadlines, penalties, exclusivity, intellectual-property transfers or material contract terms.

### Quality gates
Every deliverable must include requirements, architecture notes, source code, tests, security review, setup instructions, known limitations and reproducible evidence. Ibrahima reviews the apprentice's work; an independent quality/security agent should verify high-impact changes before release.

### Success metrics
Working prototypes delivered; test pass rate; security findings; delivery time; customer acceptance; paid missions won; realized revenue; refunds/rework; maintenance burden.

## C. Competitions, prizes and rewards department

### Proposed team
- **Competition Scout**: searches legitimate hackathons, innovation challenges, coding competitions, startup grants, business-plan competitions, creator contests, research prizes and platform reward programs.
- **Eligibility Analyst**: checks Senegal eligibility, age/residency rules, registration dates, language, required documents, team rules and geographic restrictions.
- **Rules and Risk Auditor**: checks official rules, entry fees, privacy terms, intellectual-property clauses, tax implications, prize conditions and scam indicators.
- **Submission Builder**: prepares entries, prototypes, pitch decks, forms and supporting documents.
- **Deadline and Results Tracker**: tracks milestones, submissions, judging dates, results and prize collection requirements.

### Participation policy
- Prioritize free-entry opportunities with credible organizers, transparent rules and meaningful prizes.
- Paid entry, travel, equipment, deposits or other expenses require owner approval.
- Never falsify identity, residence, age, achievements, team membership or eligibility.
- Never submit plagiarized work or claim ownership of third-party intellectual property.
- Review IP assignment, exclusivity, publicity rights, data use and prize-payment terms before entry.
- Do not submit a binding declaration or accept an award contract without required approval.
- Record official source, rules version, deadlines, eligibility evidence and submission proof.

### Key metrics
Eligible opportunities found; free-entry ratio; submissions completed; shortlist/finalist rate; prizes won and actually received; cost per entry; hours invested; net value; compliance incidents.

## Shared governance and rollout

- Register every new agent with a unique identity, supervisor, mission, permissions, status and evaluation history.
- Default to research/draft mode until access and execution permissions are explicitly granted.
- Use least-privilege access and auditable task records.
- No agent may independently withdraw, transfer, pay, purchase, or incur financial obligations.
- No agent may deploy publicly, activate production integrations, or use paid APIs without the required approval.
- Start with a research-only pilot for all three activities. Measure quality and opportunity value before granting execution permissions.
- Treat this proposal as an addition to the roadmap, not as proof that the departments or agents already exist or are operational.
